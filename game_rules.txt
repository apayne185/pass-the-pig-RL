=============================================
         PASS THE PIGS - GAME RULES
=============================================

OBJECTIVE:
  Be the first player to reach 100 points!

BASIC GAMEPLAY:
  Players take turns rolling two pig dice. On your turn, you can:
  - ROLL - Toss the pigs and score points (or risk losing them)
  - PASS - Bank your turn score and end your turn
  - HOG CALL - Make a risky bet when passing (optional)

=============================================
SCORING - WHAT THE PIGS CAN LAND ON:
=============================================

INSTANT LOSS - Your Turn Ends:
  - Pig Out (opposite sides)                →  0 pts, turn ends
  - Oinker (both on "Oinker" position)      →  Lose ALL banked points, turn ends
  - Piggyback (pigs touching)               →  GAME OVER, you lose!

Pig Positions
  - Sider (on its side)                     →  1 point
  - Double Sider                            →  1 point

  - Razorback (on its back)                 →  5 points
  - Trotter (standing on hoofs)             →  5 points

  - Snouter (snout + two forehoofs)         → 10 points
  - Double Razorback                        → 10 points
  - Double Trotter                          → 10 points

  - Leaning Jowler (jowl + ear + hoof)      → 15 points
  - Double Snouter                          → 20 points
  - Double Leaning Jowler                   → 30 points

Mixed Combos:
  Any combination adds up. Examples:
  - Razorback (5) + Snouter (10)            → 15 points
  - Trotter (5) + Leaning Jowler (15)       → 20 points

=============================================
TURN MECHANICS:
=============================================

YOUR TURN:
  1. Roll the pigs to score points
  2. Points accumulate in your "turn score" (not banked yet)
  3. Keep rolling to build your turn score
  4. PASS to bank your turn score (add to total)
  5. If you roll Pig Out, Oinker, or Piggyback → lose turn!

IMPORTANT:
  - Turn scores are NOT safe until you PASS
  - You can keep rolling as long as you don't get a bad outcome
  - First to reach 100 points wins immediately

=============================================
HOG CALLING - ADVANCED STRATEGY
=============================================

What is it?
  A risky bet you can make when passing your turn.
  You predict what your opponent will roll next!

How to Hog Call:
  When passing, choose:
  - HOG CALL 1 - Bet opponent rolls exactly 5 points
  - HOG CALL 2 - Bet opponent rolls exactly 10 points

If You're CORRECT:
  > Opponent loses DOUBLE the points (2× score)
  > You gain DOUBLE the points (2× score)
  > Opponent's turn ends immediately
  > Turn passes back to you

If You're WRONG:
  > You lose DOUBLE the points (2× score)
  > Opponent gains DOUBLE the points (2× score)
  > Opponent continues rolling with doubled points!

If Opponent Rolls Badly (Pig Out/Oinker):
  - Hog call is cancelled, no one wins the bet
  - Normal bad roll rules apply

Strategy Tips:
  - Score of 5 is common (~31% chance)
  - Score of 10 is less common (~12% chance)
  - Use when behind to catch up quickly
  - Risk: opponent can snowball with doubled points!

=============================================
WINNING THE GAME:
=============================================

Standard Rules (Rules B):
  - First player to reach 100+ points WINS immediately
  - Game ends as soon as any player hits the goal
  - No final round, instant victory!

=============================================
QUICK REFERENCE - CONSOLE COMMANDS:
=============================================

During Your Turn:
  r  →  Roll the pigs
  p  →  Pass (bank your turn score)
  1  →  Hog Call 1 (bet on 5 points)
  2  →  Hog Call 2 (bet on 10 points)
  h  →  Help (view these rules anytime)
  q  →  Quit game
